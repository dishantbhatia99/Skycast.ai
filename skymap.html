Accuracy note

SkyCast 360 is an original, atmospheric sky visualisation—not Stellarium, not a verified star catalogue, and not a real photograph or exact planetarium. Sun direction and sunrise/sunset data are location/date-based; stars and moon are illustrative. Actual sky colour depends on local pollution, terrain, clouds, buildings and camera exposure.
