Time travel: pick any date to see how the whole sky will look (or looked). Time is in your phone's time zone.
Gyroscope tips: hold the phone upright, move it in a figure-8 once to calibrate the compass, then press 🎯 with the Sun, Moon, Venus or a bright star in the centre ring to fix any remaining direction error (magnetic declination). Drag sideways to fine-tune.
Positions are approximate (planets about 1 arc-minute, Moon about 0.2°). Star data: d3-celestial / Hipparcos, bright-star list built in as a backup.
