Accuracy note

SkyCast Universe is an original lightweight planetarium inspired by sky-exploration apps, not Stellarium itself. Sun direction and sunrise/sunset data are location/date-based; bright stars, Moon and planets use lightweight approximate calculations rather than a full precision ephemeris. Actual sky colour depends on local pollution, terrain, clouds, buildings and camera exposure.
